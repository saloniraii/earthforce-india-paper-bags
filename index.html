The UWB story

Why these bags

  • 01
    Yesterday’s newspaper becomes today’s product
    Reusable, recyclable, biodegradable — a small step off plastic, and safer for wildlife.
  • 02
    The bags leave this room and go to work
    Residents of Nightingales Sandhyakirana sell them to HOPCOMS at Lalbagh. Your stack feeds their weekly pipeline.
  • 03
    Your fold is someone else’s rupee
    They need about 50,000 bags a week. That is the whole point of the next slide — linger on it.
